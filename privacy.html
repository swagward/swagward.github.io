Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This website does not use analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking of any kind. There are no third-party cookies set on this site.

Local storage

This site uses your browser's localStorage to remember your theme (light or dark) and background-animation preference. This data stays on your device, is never transmitted anywhere, and is used solely to make your next visit consistent with your last one.

Contact form

The contact form on this site is processed by Formspree, a third-party form-handling service. Formspree receives the name, email address, and message you submit, in order to deliver it to me. I only use this information to respond to your enquiry, and do not use it for any other purpose. See Formspree's own privacy policy for details on how they handle submitted data.

Questions

If you have any questions about this policy, feel free to reach out via the contact page.
